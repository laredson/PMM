# Knowledge Governance

Uploaded frequency is evidence, not proof.

Track separately:
- safety confidence;
- schema confidence;
- positive runtime evidence;
- negative runtime evidence;
- environment diversity;
- game-build relevance;
- recency;
- trusted-validator evidence.

## Illustrative thresholds

Experimental:
- passes safety/schema checks;
- at least 3 independent positive reports OR one trusted validator result;
- no malicious payload evidence.

Stable:
- at least 10 independent positive runtime reports;
- multiple compatible environments;
- configured success ratio;
- no unresolved severe incompatibility;
- automated validation pass.

Thresholds should remain configurable.

Stable knowledge may be demoted when a game update invalidates it or strong failure evidence appears. Keep history; do not erase evidence.
