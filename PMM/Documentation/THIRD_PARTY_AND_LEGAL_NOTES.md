# Third-party and legal notes

PMM v1.1 itself is licensed under MIT (`../LICENSE`). Third-party software/data keeps its own terms. See `../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

PMM operates on files supplied by the user and files available in the user's local Palworld installation. The public PMM archive intentionally does not include Palworld cooked assets or the third-party mod PAK fixtures used during development.

The public archive also intentionally does not redistribute the proprietary Oodle runtime DLL. PMM uses the open-source repak CLI; when Oodle is required, repak handles acquisition/loading through its own mechanism.

Users remain responsible for respecting Palworld/Pocketpair terms, mod-author permissions, and the rules of any external AI/file service to which they upload an `AI_HANDOFF` package. PMM cannot grant redistribution/upload rights for third-party mod PAKs included in a user-generated handoff.
